**Native** (Swift `Sources/`): `.accessibilityLabel(` 44 files/175 matches · `.accessibilityHint(` 38/116 · `.accessibilityValue(` 4/4 · `.accessibilityAddTraits(` 31/54 · `.accessibilityRemoveTraits(` 0/0 · `.accessibilityAction(` 17/40 · `.accessibilityAdjustableAction(` 2 (confirmed via direct read after a grep-tooling artifact initially showed 0) · `.accessibilityElement(` 40/92 · `.accessibilityHidden(` 35/87 · `UIAccessibility.post(notification:` 26/64 · `.accessibilityFocused(` 15/22 · magic tap 1 explicit site + Tip/What's New references · escape 1 (`TipOverlay.swift`).

**Important interpretive note:** native's lower raw counts are EXPECTED, not by themselves evidence of a gap — SwiftUI's Form/Toggle/TextField/List/NavigationLink synthesize correct roles/traits/state automatically, while React Native requires manual `accessibilityRole`/`accessibilityState` on nearly every element. The `accessibilityRole` (567) vs. `.accessibilityAddTraits` (54) gap is mostly this framework difference, not missing semantics — the row-by-row ledger above, not the raw counts, is the reliable evidence.
