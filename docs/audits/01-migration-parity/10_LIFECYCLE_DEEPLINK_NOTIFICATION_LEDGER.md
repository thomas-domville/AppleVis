## Top risk

**LIFECYCLE-014** (missing `com.apple.developer.associated-domains` entitlement) is the root cause making LIFECYCLE-010 through -013 moot in production — universal links from applevis.com currently cannot open the app at all, and even if they did, the router only opens an in-app browser rather than routing to native content. Treat this as **one Critical finding**, not four, when triaging the repair backlog. Duplicated in the security ledger as NATIVE-019/SEC-005.
